## 3. DST notice (new finding)

Official notice posted 2026-10-22 "Daylight Saving Time comes to an end":
NA times shifted one hour LATER from the 2026-10-29 maintenance until
2026-11-01 02:00 PT (a 7:00 AM PDT boss spawned at 8:00 AM PST), then "all
World Bosses after the maintenance spawn in their usual time slots" (PT wall
clock). The Daily Reset does not move at maintenance; it stays on UTC and so
sits one hour later on the PT wall clock until the clock change. Spring 2026
had the mirror window (NA: after the 03-05 maintenance until 03-08, one hour
earlier; from search snippet, not fetched). EW's table assumes PT wall clock
always (`world_bosses_na.json`, "unverified until after 2026-11-01") and so is
wrong inside each 3-day window. The next window is spring 2027 (US clocks
change 2027-03-14, maintenance 2027-03-11). Plan 031 is DST-correct for the
long run; plan 072 only compares to a third-party table; neither models the
window. Proposed as plan 176.
